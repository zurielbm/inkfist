1 · Engine decision 技

You suggested Bevy. It works and does export to the web, but for this MVP I chose TypeScript + Three.js + Vite. Here's the comparison:

Bevy (Rust → WASM)Three.js + TypeScript ✅
First load for a player clicking an X link~8–25 MB wasm and a slow first frame~0.5 MB engine and code, plus streamed assets
glTF from Blender (skinned and animated)SupportedSupported, very mature
Custom ink and watercolour shadersWGSL, fineGLSL, fine (WebGL2 runs everywhere, including mobile Safari)
WebRTC / WebSocket netcodeNeeds extra crates and glueNative browser APIs
Iteration speed (balancing frame data)Slow compiles on WindowsHot reload in under a second
Static hosting on SubirWorksWorks (base:'./' for relative paths)

The game simulation is kept deterministic and engine-agnostic (plain TS, integer math), so a later move to Bevy or a native client stays possible.

3 · Combat: “Freestyle Flow” 流

The goal is the feel of Chinese wuxia action games (deflects, dodge-cancels, qinggong, internal-energy arts), condensed into a readable 1v1. There are no quarter-circle inputs: every technique is a direction plus a button. Depth comes from chaining freely and from a counter triangle.

Local versus P2: arrows + 1–6 on the numpad. Touch: a virtual stick plus a six-button fan.

Counter triangle 三才

Holding Guard blocks Strikes and Arts safely but gets crushed by Focus. Deflect beats everything if timed, but a whiffed Deflect is punishable. Dodge has invincible frames and can cancel a combo's recovery for half a Qi bar.

Freestyle chains

Any attack can cancel into any different attack on hit. Direction changes the move (forward lunges, up launches, down sweeps, airborne versions), so dozens of routes open up with no input lists. Repeating a move in one combo goes stale: less damage and shorter hitstun, so infinites die naturally.

Flow ranks

Each new move in a combo adds Flow, which raises the damage multiplier (up to ×1.3), Qi gain, and how loud the ink effects get:

Qi 氣 & Qinggong 輕功

Three Qi bars fill from landing hits, deflecting and taking damage. They pay for Qi Arts (1 bar), dodge-cancels (½), a Qi Burst combo breaker (2), and the cinematic Secret Art (3). Movement includes a double jump, air-dash and wall-run off the arena's cliff edges, plus meteor dives that leave craters.

7 · Multiplayer 對

Matchmaking (no server of our own)

  • Private Duel: the host gets a 5-letter room code (e.g. KWANG) and a shareable link …/inkfist/#duel=KWANG. Whoever opens it joins.
  • Quick Match: a lobby-slot protocol on the PeerJS broker. The first player to claim inkfist-q-0 waits, the next player connects to them and they're paired, then the slot is freed for the next pair. Busy and stale slots are retried with backoff.
  • The menu shows an “online now” presence count from the same broker, on a best-effort basis.
  • Version check during the handshake, so mismatched builds don't desync.

Rollback netcode (GGPO-style)

Risk: pure P2P WebRTC without a TURN relay fails on roughly 10–15% of networks (strict NAT, some mobile carriers). The game detects this and suggests “Versus AI” instead. The proper fix is a small Cloudflare relay, and I've sketched it as a drop-in transport (see Q&A 2).

Master 上~12 framesDeflects ~30% of the time, punishes whiffs, Focus vs. turtling, spends Qi on Arts
Gosu 高手~8 framesFull Flow combos, dodge-cancels, Qi Bursts, mix-ups, Secret Art finishers

The AI reads the game state through a delay buffer, so it reacts at human speed and doesn't read inputs. It only produces controller inputs, so it follows exactly the same rules as a player.

10 · Build order 序

1
Scaffold + deterministic sim. Vite/TS/Three project, fixed-step loop, fighter state machine, hitboxes, frame data and rounds, plus headless tests in Node (determinism and rollback equivalence).
2
Blender character pipeline. A shared skeleton, three character meshes (robes, hair and sleeve bone chains) and a pose-library animation set, exported to .glb with timing metadata JSON.

Ink renderer. Toon shading, ink outlines, watercolour and paper post-processing, the Blender stage renders (parallax mountains, cliff arena), craters and effects.
4
Combat feel. All moves for three fighters, Flow and stale system, deflect, Focus, dodge-cancel, Qi Arts, Secret Arts with manhwa panel cut-ins, hit-stop and shake, brush sound-effect text, procedural audio.
5
Screens. Loading, title menu, character select with Blender portraits, how-to-play, settings, HUD, pause, results.
6
AI at four difficulties, then online: PeerJS transport, private duel codes, Quick Match queue, rollback session, rematch and disconnect handling.
7
Share and support. X intent, victory-card image, OG meta and key art, tip and sponsor config.
8
Polish, test and deploy to pages.subir.dev/inkfist/: touch controls, gamepad, performance pass, smoke tests of all modes.

11 · Risks 險

RiskMitigation
Procedurally modelled characters look crudeStylised low-poly forms with strong ink outlines and flowing cloth and hair. The ink shader does the heavy lifting, as in the manhwa. I'll render previews and iterate.
Scripted animations look stiffAnticipation, smear and follow-through poses, 60 fps keys, plus procedural secondary motion on hair, sleeves and scarves.
Public PeerJS broker is down or NAT blocks P2PClear error with an AI fallback, and a transport abstraction so the Cloudflare relay can be dropped in.
Desyncs in online playInteger-only sim, no Math.sin in gameplay, seeded RNG, per-frame checksums, and a headless rollback test that compares against a non-rollback run.
Using the Gosu name or characters (IP)Only the art style is used. The name, characters and story are original.

12 · Questions for you 問

I'm already building with the default marked on each question, so none of these block progress. Reply whenever you like.

Q1. Which tip link should the “Support the Dojo” button use?
Why I'm asking: I can't create payment accounts for you, and the button has to point to a page that you own and that pays you.
Default meanwhile: a placeholder TIP_URL in src/config.ts pointing to ko-fi.com. Send me your Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors or Stripe link.
Q2. Can I deploy a small Cloudflare Worker relay to your Cloudflare account?
Why I'm asking: P2P WebRTC fails for some players behind strict NATs. A Worker + Durable Object relay (free tier) also makes Quick Match more reliable than the public PeerJS broker. It needs you to run npx wrangler login once, which is an interactive browser sign-in I can't do for you.
Default meanwhile: PeerJS P2P only. The relay code will be in server/, ready to deploy.
Q3. Is the name “INKFIST 墨拳” and the URL pages.subir.dev/inkfist/ OK?
Why I'm asking: the name goes on the logo, the X posts and the shared link, and changing it after people share it breaks their links. I avoided “Gosu” because it's the manhwa's title.
Default meanwhile: INKFIST at /inkfist/. Your Subir convention would prefix it as ma-inkfist, but you asked for a clean shareable name, so I dropped the prefix.
Q4. Do you have a real sponsor (name, logo, URL) for the ad slot?
Why I'm asking: the slot looks better with real content, and I need to know whether it should show an image banner or text.
Default meanwhile: a text slot reading “Sponsor this dojo — your brand here” that links to your tip page.
Q5. How important are phones for the first test?
Why I'm asking: many people will open the X link on a phone. Touch controls for a fighting game take real tuning time, which would otherwise go to more moves or a fourth fighter.
Default meanwhile: a basic virtual stick and buttons, keyboard and gamepad first.
